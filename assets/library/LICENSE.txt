Klipolo pack: 35 sound effects, 4 music beds, 43 stickers and 8 backgrounds.

Klipolo made the music beds, the backgrounds and these stickers from code (scripts/gen-library.py), with no recordings,
samples or outside media: arrow, circle, bubble, arrow-curved, arrow-pointer, circle-drawn, underline, number-1,
number-2, number-3, number-4, number-5, thought-bubble, shout-bubble, new-tag, subscribe, cursor-hand.

They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
