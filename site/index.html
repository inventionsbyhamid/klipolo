The video editor you um, just talk to.

Klipolo is a Mac app that edits video from a chat box. Drop in raw footage and say what you want. Every cut comes back as a proposal you approve.

Download for Apple Silicon Download for Intel

Free while in beta. Bring your own Meta API key. First build Nov 4, 2026. macOS 15 or later.

Editing is the slow part.

  • You record in OBS, then open a second app to edit.
  • Your recorder has no zoom on clicks, so viewers squint at tiny text.
  • A 20 minute recording means scrubbing by hand for every um, pause and retake.
  • Captions and a vertical cut for Shorts or Reels mean another tool and another hour.

Klipolo does the cuts, captions and vertical version in one place. You just say what you want.

Built on Meta's models

Klipolo uses four of Meta's AI models, and no others. Three run through Meta's model API. Meta's open MMS aligner runs on Klipolo's server.

Muse Spark
Watches your footage, picture and sound, and turns your chat into edits.
Muse Voice Transcribe
Turns what people say into a transcript, split by speaker.
Muse Image
Draws stickers and title card backgrounds when you ask.
MMS aligner
Times every word, so captions follow the voice and cuts land between words.

Where your footage goes

Most of the work happens on your Mac. Some of it needs Meta's models.

Happens on your Mac

  • Editing
  • Export

Sent to Meta's model API

  • The audio
  • Low-resolution copies of the video
  • Your chat messages, with the project's name, transcript, scene log and file names
  • What you ask Muse Image to draw

Sent to Klipolo's server

  • The audio and transcript, to time each word
  • Your requests to Meta, when you use an access code

Klipolo sends these so Meta's models can understand your footage and do what you ask. Klipolo's server keeps no audio, video or text, only each call's usage and cost, and each Mac's word timing minutes for the day. Because of all this, Klipolo needs an internet connection.

Questions

What does it cost?

Klipolo is free while in beta. The AI features use your own Meta API key, so Meta bills that key for what the models use. Editing and export work without one.

When can I download it?

The first build lands Nov 4, 2026, for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 15 or later.

Will it cut anything without asking?

No. Anything that removes footage comes back as a proposal, with the parts to cut in red. You approve it first. Undo is one step.

Does it work offline?

No. Editing and export happen on your Mac. The AI features send the audio, low-resolution copies of the video, your chat messages with the project's name, transcript, scene log and file names, and what you ask Muse Image to draw to Meta's model API. The audio and transcript also go to Klipolo's server to time each word, and with an access code your requests to Meta pass through it. The server keeps no audio, video or text, only each call's usage and cost, and each Mac's word timing minutes for the day.

Which languages do captions support?

English first. You can fix any word by hand.

Is Klipolo made by Meta?

No. Klipolo is built on Meta's models. Meta did not make it and does not endorse it.
